Manual (human player)

You are the keysmith block. Reach the door — it opens only when your key's shape, color and rotation all match the pattern drawn on the door.
Mechanics
Rotator ✚ — step on it: key rotation +90°.
Shape changer ◇ — cycles the key's shape.
Color changer ▦ — cycles the key's color.
Energy pellet ● — refills your step budget (single-use).
Piston ⇥ — shoves you several cells until you hit a wall.
Portal ◎ — teleports you to the paired portal.
Every action costs 1 energy. Energy 0 ⇒ level resets (key & pellets restored). Changers re-trigger every time you enter the tile — bounce on/off to cycle repeatedly.
Controls Arrow keys / WASD / buttons. Z undo · R reset level.
